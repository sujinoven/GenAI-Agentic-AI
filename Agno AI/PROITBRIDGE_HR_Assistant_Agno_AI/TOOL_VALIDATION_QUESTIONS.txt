FLAGSHIP — THE FULL RESIGNATION REQUEST  (-> all four HR specialists + synthesis)
1. I am planning to resign from PROITBRIDGE. Based on my role, notice period,
   leave balance, salary, pending reimbursements and company policies, calculate
   my final settlement, identify everything I need to complete before my last
   working day, and prepare my resignation and HR handover documents.


HR POLICY AGENT   (Team -> HR Policy Agent -> Handbook)
1. What is the notice period for a confirmed employee?
2. How is Earned Leave encashed on separation?
3. Can I take leave during my notice period?
4. What are the resignation steps in the handbook?


PAYROLL AGENT   (Team -> Payroll Agent -> record + calculator + handbook)
1. Calculate my full and final settlement.
2. Calculate my Earned Leave encashment.
3. What is my notice shortfall buy-out if I serve 45 days instead of 60?
4. What is my final-month salary for 19 days worked?


COMPLIANCE AGENT   (Team -> Compliance Agent -> Handbook + record)
1. What must I complete before my last working day?
2. Which clearances do I need, and which assets do I return?
3. When will I receive my relieving letter?


DOCUMENTATION AGENT   (Team -> Documentation Agent)
1. Draft my resignation letter to my reporting manager.
2. Prepare my handover checklist.
3. Write a short HR communication email about my exit.


HR RESEARCH AGENT   (Team -> HR Research Agent -> web; external only)
1. What is the typical notice period in the Indian IT industry?
2. How does Form 16 / PF withdrawal generally work?


GENERAL HR — the 7 tools, beyond the resignation
1. How much Earned Leave will I accrue after 6 completed months?   (calculate_earned_leave_accrual -> 9)
